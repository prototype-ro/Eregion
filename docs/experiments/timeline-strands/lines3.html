Timeline energy: continuous light, no particles

The lines themselves carry the energy. A shader paints a hot core, a smooth falloff and slow waves of brightness that travel along each strand, and the light spills onto the dot grid around it. Click a dot or a branch name, hover a dot, or press "Simulate save". Compare with the particle version.

"Reduced motion" keeps the colour and glow but stops the waves. The shader runs once per pixel on the graphics card, so the cost does not grow with the number of branches.
